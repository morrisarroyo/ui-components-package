# Button

A clickable button that triggers an action. It comes in two visual styles and
two sizes, and can show that it is busy or unavailable.

## Variants

Use **primary** for the main action on a screen, and **secondary** for
everything else.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Variants} />

| Variant | Background | Text | Border | Hover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `primary` | color.primary | white | — | color.primary.hover |
| `secondary` | color.surface | color.text | color.border | color.surface.subtle |

## Sizes

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Sizes} />

| Size | Vertical padding | Horizontal padding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | space.1 | space.3 |
| `md` (default) | space.2 | space.4 |

Both sizes use radius.sm and font.button.

## Loading

A spinner replaces the label and the button is non-interactive. Its width does
not change.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Loading} />

## Disabled

color.disabled.bg background, color.disabled.text text, and non-interactive.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Disabled} />

## Accessibility

- Works from the keyboard: Tab to it, activate with Enter or Space.
- Keyboard focus shows a 2px outline in color.focus with a 2px offset.
- Disabled is the native `disabled` attribute, so it is actually disabled.
