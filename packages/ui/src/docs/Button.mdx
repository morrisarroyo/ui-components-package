## Variants

**Primary** for the screen's main action; **secondary** for the rest.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Variants} />

| Variant | Background | Text | Border | Hover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `primary` | color.primary | white | — | color.primary.hover |
| `secondary` | color.surface | color.text | color.border | color.surface.subtle |

## Sizes

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Sizes} />

| Size | Vertical padding | Horizontal padding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | space.1 | space.3 |
| `md` (default) | space.2 | space.4 |

Both sizes use radius.sm and font.button.

## Loading

A spinner replaces the label, at the same width. Clicks are ignored.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Loading} />

## Disabled

Greyed out (color.disabled.bg, color.disabled.text) and can't be clicked or focused.

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Disabled} />

## Accessibility

- Tab to it; press Enter or Space to activate.
- Keyboard focus shows a color.focus ring inside a color.primary ring, to meet
  the 3:1 contrast rule.
- Disabled is truly disabled (the native `disabled` attribute).
- Loading is announced as busy, and the button keeps keyboard focus.
